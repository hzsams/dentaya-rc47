BodyParts3D 4.0, official 99% reduction archive, binary conversion by OMFAtlas (https://github.com/choxos/OMFAtlas). DentAYA centers and scales individual models for schematic tooth selection. Third molars are representative resized second molars. These are anatomical reference models, not patient scans.

RC24 display variants: optional width/depth/height adjustments of the reference crowns for visual selection only. Not patient anatomy or restoration geometry.
