Included: original 601, 605, 606, 608 STL files and their JSON annotations, copied from 601-650.zip. Original STL bytes unchanged; source archive MD5 and included SHA256 verified. See public/training-scans/provenance.json and selected-metadata.csv.

The publisher describes these as cleaned and standardized surface meshes from clinical intraoral scans. These are research surface records, not ready-to-manufacture restorations. DentAYA adds fictional demo labels and manual workflow cards; those labels are not identities of the source patients. No clinical correspondence between the fictional person and original scanned person is asserted.

Source labels:
601: scan body, 46.
605: scan bodies, 15 14 13 23 24 25.
606: scan bodies, 47 44.
608: crowns and scan bodies in the upper arch; see selected-metadata.csv.

No matching crown-preparation or fully edentulous denture scan is included for DEMO-005/006/007. Those scenarios are explicitly marked as awaiting appropriate scan files. No artificial scan is represented as a real scan.
