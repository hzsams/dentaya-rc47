# RC32 translation-generation attribution

English-Irish and English-Maltese corrections used HPLT MT v2.0 (HPLT Consortium, University of Edinburgh and Charles University in Prague), CC BY 4.0, alongside OPUS-MT fallback and developer terminology corrections. Model repository revisions and original model cards are recorded here. HPLT models were trained with HPLT v2.0 and OPUS data. No model weights are included in this application source package. Earlier OPUS-MT / M2M100 provenance is retained in verification/RC31/model-provenance. Japanese corrections used M2M100; bilingual OPUS-MT models and M2M100 were used for other language corrections. All inference ran locally. Native-language editorial approval is not claimed.

HPLT model repositories: https://huggingface.co/HPLT/translate-en-ga-v2.0-hplt_opus and https://huggingface.co/HPLT/translate-en-mt-v2.0-hplt_opus
